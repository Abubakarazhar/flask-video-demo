# Section 1: Product Customer Brief

## Problem

Factory safety incidents are preventable, yet they continue to occur at alarming rates. The core issue is not lack of safety protocols, but this:

**Safety violations and hazards are detected too late—after incidents occur or during reactive inspections—when intervention is most expensive and least effective.**

### In Real-World Settings:

- **Safety officers** cannot monitor all areas simultaneously
- **Workers** may forget or skip PPE due to routine complacency
- **Managers** only discover violations during scheduled inspections
- **Incidents** happen in blind spots between patrols
- **Compliance** is measured reactively, not proactively

### The Core Problem:

**There is no continuous, intelligent monitoring system that detects safety violations in real-time and provides early warnings before incidents occur.**

### Consequences of This Problem:

- **Late detection** (after violations occur)
- **Reactive compliance** (fixing problems after they happen)
- **High incident costs** (injuries, downtime, insurance)
- **Compliance gaps** (missed violations between inspections)
- **Worker safety at risk** (preventable accidents)

### Organizations (factories, warehouses, construction sites) are willing to pay because:

- **Preventing incidents** is far cheaper than responding to them
- **OSHA compliance** requires continuous monitoring
- **Insurance premiums** decrease with better safety records
- **Worker retention** improves with safer environments
- **Productivity** increases when workers feel safe

## Our Unique Value Proposition (That Fills the Market Gap)

**Our product is an intelligent, continuous safety monitoring system that detects violations and hazards in real-time using AI vision-language models, not a simple rule-based camera system.**

### What the Product Actually Does:

1. **Continuous Video Analysis**
   - Monitors factory floor 24/7
   - Analyzes frames using advanced AI vision models
   - Detects people, PPE, equipment, and hazards

2. **Contextual Risk Assessment**
   - Understands **why** something is unsafe (not just **what**)
   - Considers location, activity, and environmental context
   - Distinguishes between safe and unsafe scenarios

3. **Early Warning System**
   - Detects violations **before** incidents occur
   - Identifies patterns and trends over time
   - Provides actionable recommendations

4. **Intelligent Alerting**
   - Deduplicates similar alerts (prevents spam)
   - Prioritizes by severity (critical vs. minor)
   - Explains what changed and why it matters

### What Makes This Fundamentally Different:

- **We do not** just detect hard hats (simple rule)
- **We do not** require workers to wear devices
- **We do not** replace safety officers
- **We do not** just record video for later review

**Instead, we answer a new question:**

**"Is this scene safe given the context, and what specific risks are present right now?"**

### The Opportunity:

- **Shift safety from reactive → preventive**
- **Enable continuous monitoring** without dedicated personnel
- **Reduce false alarms** through intelligent analysis
- **Provide actionable insights** for safety officers
- **Create a new category**: Intelligent Safety Monitoring

## Minimum Hardware Specifications

**No dedicated hardware is required for the MVP.**

The MVP is designed as a software-only system, operating on:

- **User's existing infrastructure** (servers, cloud instances)
- **Standard video sources** (IP cameras, video files, webcams)
- **Cloud or on-premises deployment**

### Justification:

The core hypothesis is that safety violations can be detected from video analysis using AI vision-language models, not specialized hardware.

Requiring hardware would:
- **Increase friction** (installation complexity)
- **Reduce adoption** (cost barrier)
- **Limit flexibility** (vendor lock-in)
- **Distract from validating the core idea**

### Optional (NOT required for MVP):

- **IP camera integration** (RTSP streams)
- **Multiple camera support** (future phase)
- **Edge device deployment** (future phase)

---

## Minimum AI Specifications

The AI stack must support **contextual scene understanding**, not just object detection.

### Core AI Capabilities (Required)

#### 1. **Vision-Language Model (VLM) Integration**
- **Scene understanding** (what is happening?)
- **Object detection** (people, equipment, PPE)
- **Context awareness** (where, when, why)
- **Text recognition** (safety signs, labels)

#### 2. **Risk Reasoning Engine**
- **Contextual risk assessment** (is this safe given the context?)
- **Severity classification** (LOW, MEDIUM, HIGH, CRITICAL)
- **Actionable recommendations** (what should be done?)

#### 3. **Temporal Pattern Detection**
- **Event aggregation** over time windows
- **Trend identification** (recurring issues, escalation)
- **Pattern recognition** (unsafe behaviors, violations)

#### 4. **Explainability**
- **Clear explanations** of why something is unsafe
- **Contextual reasoning** (not just "hard hat missing")
- **Human-readable alerts** (not technical jargon)

### AI Methods (MVP-appropriate)

#### Primary:
- **OpenAI GPT-4 Vision** (or GPT-4o) for scene understanding
- **OpenAI GPT-4** (or GPT-4o) for risk reasoning
- **Rule-based aggregation** for temporal patterns
- **Semantic similarity** for alert deduplication

#### Fallback (Mock Mode):
- **Basic computer vision** (OpenCV) for testing
- **Heuristic reasoning** for risk assessment
- **Color/shape detection** for PPE identification

### LLM Usage (Controlled, Purposeful)

- **Vision analysis**: Frame → structured safety observations
- **Risk reasoning**: Observations → contextual risk assessment
- **Explanation generation**: Technical data → human-readable alerts
- **No diagnosis**: No medical or psychological claims
- **No therapy**: No mental health recommendations
