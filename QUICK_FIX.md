## 📹 Get a Real Factory Video

### Easiest Way (Recommended):

1. **Go to Pexels** (Free, Real Videos):
   - https://www.pexels.com/search/factory/
   - Or: https://www.pexels.com/search/warehouse/

2. **Download any factory video** (click Download button)

3. **Save it** in this folder as `factory_video.mp4`

4. **Upload it** through the web interface at http://localhost:8080

### Or Use Your Own Video:

- Any factory/warehouse/industrial video works!
- Just upload it through the web interface
- Formats: MP4, AVI, MOV, MKV, WebM
