1. **Visit:** https://www.pexels.com/search/factory/
   - Or: https://www.pexels.com/search/warehouse/

2. **Choose a video** that shows:
   - Factory floor
   - Workers (preferably)
   - Machinery/equipment
   - Industrial setting

3. **Download it:**
   - Click the video
   - Click "Download" button (free, no signup)
   - Save it

### Step 2: Use It
1. **Place video** in this folder: `factory_safety_monitoring/`
2. **Or upload** through web interface at http://localhost:8080
3. **Click "Start Analysis"**
