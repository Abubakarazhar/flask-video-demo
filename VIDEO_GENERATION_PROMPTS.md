# 🎬 AI Video Generation Prompts for Factory Safety Monitoring

## 🎯 **Best Prompt (Recommended):**

```
A realistic factory floor surveillance camera view showing an industrial manufacturing facility. 
Wide-angle shot of a modern factory floor with:
- Gray concrete floor with yellow safety line markings
- Large industrial machinery in the background (CNC machines, conveyor belts)
- Two factory workers walking around the floor
- One worker wearing a yellow hard hat and orange safety vest (compliant)
- One worker without a hard hat, only wearing a blue work shirt (safety violation)
- Overhead industrial lighting creating realistic shadows
- Safety equipment visible (fire extinguisher on wall, first aid station)
- Warning signs and safety markings on the floor
- Realistic factory environment with pipes, equipment, and industrial infrastructure
- Professional surveillance camera angle, steady shot
- Natural lighting, realistic colors
- Duration: 30 seconds, smooth camera movement or static shot
```

## 📋 **Prompt Tips:**

**For Best Results:**
- ✅ Include "surveillance camera" or "security camera" for realistic angle
- ✅ Specify "realistic" or "photorealistic"
- ✅ Mention specific safety violations (missing hard hat, etc.)
- ✅ Include lighting details ("industrial lighting", "overhead lights")
- ✅ Specify camera angle ("wide-angle", "overhead", "security camera view")

**What to Include:**
- ✅ Workers (some with PPE, some without)
- ✅ Factory/warehouse environment
- ✅ Machinery/equipment
- ✅ Safety equipment visible
- ✅ Realistic setting
